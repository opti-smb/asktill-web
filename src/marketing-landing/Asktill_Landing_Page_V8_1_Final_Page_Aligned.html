About the founder

Meet the person building Asktill.

Founder & CEO, Asktill

Click the name to read the full story

Mandeep Chaudhary is the Founder & CEO of AskTill, bringing more than 25 years of experience across banking, risk, lending and fintech.

His career has given him a rare view of small-business finance from both sides of the table — first inside global financial institutions including Citi and Barclays, and later as an entrepreneur building digital lending businesses for SMEs.

Mandeep went on to build, scale and exit two digital lending platforms, including KNAB Finance, focused on SME cash-flow lending, and Abhi Loans, a fully digital lending platform. Together, these businesses served more than 50,000 customers and used technology, data and AI/ML to make lending faster and more intelligent.

But working closely with thousands of businesses revealed a deeper problem.

Many good businesses did not lack ambition, customers or even profitability. They lacked financial clarity.

Owners often had bank accounts, accounting systems, payment platforms and spreadsheets full of information — yet still struggled to answer the questions that mattered most:

Where is my cash going? Why are my margins changing? What needs my attention today? And what should I do next?

That experience became the inspiration for AskTill.

Mandeep is now building AskTill to give small-business owners something large enterprises have long enjoyed: clear, intelligent and actionable financial decision support — without the complexity.

His belief is simple:

Business owners shouldn't have to become finance experts to understand their own business.

AskTill is being built to make that possible.
