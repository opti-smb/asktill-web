ASKTILL SECURITY & TRUST
Last Updated: August 23, 2026
Your business financial information deserves strong protection.
AskTill is being built with security and privacy as core product requirements.
Because AskTill is an early-stage platform, our security program will continue to mature as the product, integrations and customer base grow.
This page explains our current security principles and the controls AskTill is building around them.
Protecting Business Financial Information
AskTill may process sensitive business information from banks, payment processors, POS systems, e-commerce platforms and other sources.
Our security approach is based on four principles:
Minimize access. Protect what we process. Monitor our systems. Respond quickly when something goes wrong.
1. Secure Financial Connections
Where AskTill connects with financial institutions through providers such as Plaid or other authorized connectivity providers, AskTill uses the provider's supported connection process.
AskTill is designed so that bank-login credentials entered within a third-party provider's connection interface are handled by that provider rather than used by AskTill to independently log into your financial institution.
AskTill receives only the information authorized for the applicable connection and AskTill service.
Users can disconnect supported financial connections when they no longer want AskTill to retrieve information from them.
2. Encryption
AskTill uses industry-standard encryption designed to protect sensitive information in transit and at rest, where applicable. Specific technical controls may vary by system, data type, infrastructure provider, and security requirements.
Sensitive information stored by AskTill should also be protected using encryption appropriate to the data and infrastructure involved.
Encryption keys and application secrets should be appropriately protected and access restricted.
3. Access Controls
Access to sensitive production information is intended to be restricted to authorized personnel who require access for a legitimate business or technical purpose.
AskTill's access-control approach includes:
least-privilege access;
role-based permissions where appropriate;
restricted production access;
controlled administrative privileges;
secure credential management; and
removal of access when it is no longer required.
Access to particularly sensitive systems should use strong authentication controls.
4. Infrastructure Security
AskTill uses established cloud and technology providers to operate its Service.
Our infrastructure security approach is designed around:
secure configuration;
environment separation;
network protections;
restricted administrative access;
secrets management;
security logging;
backups where appropriate; and
monitoring of important production systems.
Using a major cloud provider does not by itself make an application secure. AskTill remains responsible for securely configuring and operating its own systems.
5. Data Minimization
A central AskTill security principle is:
Don't keep information simply because we can.
AskTill aims to retrieve and retain only information reasonably required to deliver the selected services.
Where possible, AskTill seeks to limit:
unnecessary collection;
unnecessary duplication;
unnecessary employee access;
unnecessary retention; and
unnecessary transfer of Customer information.
6. Secure Development
AskTill's software-development practices are intended to incorporate security throughout the development lifecycle.
This includes practices such as:
reviewing significant code changes;
managing software dependencies;
protecting secrets and API credentials;
separating development and production environments;
testing important functionality;
applying security patches;
monitoring known vulnerabilities; and
addressing security issues based on risk.
7. Vulnerability Management
AskTill seeks to identify and remediate vulnerabilities based on their severity and potential impact.
This includes vulnerabilities identified through:
internal testing;
dependency and infrastructure monitoring;
service providers;
security tools; and
responsible disclosures from security researchers.
AskTill maintains a separate Vulnerability Disclosure Policy describing how security issues may be reported.
Security vulnerabilities can be reported to:
support@asktill.com
8. Logging and Monitoring
AskTill's systems may maintain security and operational logs to help:
detect unauthorized access;
investigate unusual activity;
troubleshoot problems;
identify attempted misuse;
investigate security incidents; and
maintain system reliability.
Access to security logs should be appropriately controlled.
AskTill does not intentionally use security logging as a means to collect unnecessary Customer financial information.
9. Incident Response
AskTill maintains or is developing procedures for identifying, investigating, containing and responding to security incidents.
Depending on the circumstances, incident response may include:
detection and assessment;
containment;
investigation;
remediation;
credential or key rotation;
restoration;
monitoring;
notification where legally or contractually required; and
review of lessons learned.
If AskTill determines that applicable law requires notification of Customers or authorities, AskTill will provide such notification as required.
10. Third-Party Providers
AskTill relies on third-party technology providers to deliver certain functions.
These may include providers for:
cloud infrastructure;
financial connectivity;
authentication;
payments;
communications;
analytics;
support services; and
artificial intelligence.
AskTill seeks to select reputable providers and limit their access to information to what is reasonably required for their services.
Third-party providers are responsible for the security of their own systems, while AskTill remains responsible for how those providers are incorporated into AskTill's service.
11. Employee and Contractor Access
People are part of security.
AskTill seeks to limit access to sensitive Customer information to personnel who have a legitimate need for it.
Personnel with access to sensitive systems or information are expected to:
maintain confidentiality;
use secure authentication;
protect credentials;
follow AskTill security requirements; and
report suspected incidents.
12. Business Continuity and Backups
AskTill's infrastructure strategy is designed to reduce the risk that a single technical failure causes unnecessary data loss or prolonged service interruption.
Depending on the relevant system, safeguards may include backups, redundancy and recovery procedures.
AskTill does not guarantee uninterrupted availability of the Service.
13. Customer Responsibilities
Security is shared.
Customers should:
use strong and unique passwords;
protect account credentials;
restrict AskTill access to authorized team members;
maintain security on their own computers and devices;
remove access when employees or advisers leave;
monitor Connected Accounts;
promptly report suspicious activity; and
avoid sending sensitive credentials through email or support channels.
14. Privacy
Our security practices work alongside our privacy practices.
For information about the types of information AskTill processes and how it is used, please review the AskTill Privacy Policy.
15. Compliance and Security Certifications
AskTill is an early-stage technology company.
AskTill does not claim a security certification, regulatory approval or independent assurance report unless that certification or report has actually been obtained and is expressly identified by AskTill.
In particular, references to industry security standards or practices should not be interpreted as a representation that AskTill is certified under:
SOC 2;
ISO 27001;
PCI DSS;
GLBA;
or any other regulatory or certification framework,
unless AskTill expressly states otherwise.
As AskTill's platform and customer requirements grow, we expect our security and assurance program to evolve accordingly.
AskTill may update this page if and when a certification, independent assurance report, or other formal security attestation is actually obtained.
