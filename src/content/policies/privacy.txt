ASKTILL PRIVACY POLICY
Last Updated: August 23, 2026
AskTill Inc. ("AskTill," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, protect and retain information when you visit AskTill.com, create an AskTill account, connect business financial accounts, upload information or otherwise use AskTill's products and services.
AskTill is designed primarily for businesses and business users in the United States.
By using AskTill, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

The information we collect depends on how you use AskTill.

We may collect information such as:
name;

telephone number;
job title or role;

We may collect information about your business, including:

information needed to configure AskTill for your business.

AskTill may allow you to connect third-party services such as:
bank accounts;
payment processors;
POS systems;

e-commerce platforms; and
other business systems.

When you connect an account through a third-party provider, that provider may collect information directly from you under its own privacy policy and terms.
AskTill is designed so that online banking credentials used within third-party connection interfaces are handled by the applicable connectivity provider rather than used by AskTill to independently log into your bank account.
We do not use your banking credentials to initiate transactions unless AskTill introduces a separate feature that you expressly authorize and that feature is governed by appropriate additional terms.

spreadsheets; and
other business records.

AskTill may generate or infer information from data provided to the Service, including:
financial summaries;
reconciliations;
transaction classifications;
possible missing transactions;
anomalies;
business-health indicators;
cash-flow trends;
forecasts;
potential chargebacks;
possible recoveries;

recommendations; and
other AskTill-generated insights.

device type;
operating system;
pages viewed;
referring pages;

AskTill may use information to:

AskTill does not sell Customer financial data for the purpose of training unrelated third-party AI models.
